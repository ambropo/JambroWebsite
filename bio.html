Bio

Ambrogio Cesa-Bianchi is a Senior Advisor at the Bank of England and a Research Fellow at the CEPR (IMF and ME programmes). He is an Associate Editor at the European Economic Review and the Journal of Applied Econometrics, and a Guest Lecturer at the London School of Economics.

His research is in macroeconomics, monetary policy, and international macro & finance, with a focus on credit, uncertainty, global financial conditions, and the international transmission of shocks. His work has appeared in the Review of Financial Studies, the Journal of International Economics, the American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics, the European Economic Review, and the Journal of Money, Credit and Banking, among others.
